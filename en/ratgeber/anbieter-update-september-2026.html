Vendor Update September 2026: What Changed Since Our First Review

Nine days after our first review, we checked all 24 vendors again. Domains change hands, legal notices get filled in, shipping areas shrink. The overview, sourced and dated.

How we re-checked

On 06.09.2026 we systematically recorded 24 research-peptide vendors for the first time: legal notices, lab verification, shipping areas, publicly viewable certificates of analysis (CoA). Nine days later, on 15.09.2026, we checked the same 24 vendors again, page by page, source by source. This update shows what actually changed in that short window, with a date and a source for every claim.

The sources are publicly verifiable: the vendor websites themselves, the Internet Archive (Wayback Machine), review platforms such as Trustpilot and ScamAdviser, and the independent comparison platform ChemVerify. Where a claim rests only on a community source such as Reddit, we label it explicitly as an observation, not a confirmed fact. The full method behind our scores is on the methodology page; the current status of all 24 vendors is in the comparison.

Vanished and redirected shops

The most striking finding concerns Avellon Peptides. The last confirmed snapshot from 24.08.2026 (Wayback Machine) still shows the familiar Avellon shop. As of 15.09.2026, the domain avellon-peptides.com, and the hyphen-free spelling as well, redirects entirely to a different shop called Smart Peptides (smartpeptides.de, operator per its legal notice: Aktru Sp. z o.o., Poland). smartpeptides.de carries no mention of Avellon anywhere. Whether this is a sale, a rebrand, or a third-party domain takeover could not be established. The Avellon site itself is no longer reachable for regular visitors and is marked inactive in our database; see the Avellon Peptides vendor page for details.

SERA Lab moved in a different direction: as of 06.09.2026 the domain still redirected to changing third-party shops; today (15.09.2026, double-checked via curl) it shows only an empty Hostinger parking page with no shop content at all. The encrypted HTTPS version is not reachable at all; only the unencrypted HTTP version serves the parking page. ScamAdviser gives the domain a trust score of 13/100, explicitly noting that the content could not be analyzed.

Back online, but not without questions

As of 06.09.2026, Driada was classified as possibly dead. On 15.09.2026 the shop at driadashop.to is demonstrably active, with a working catalog. Also newly confirmed is a sitewide RUO notice in the footer, which our baseline had still marked absent. At the same time, the current check surfaces two new risk signals: ScamAdviser rates the site 0/100, 'Very Likely Unsafe', while Gridinsoft scores it 42/100. The catalog also remains structurally weighted toward anabolic compounds.

Peptide Shop Deutschland shows a similar pattern: the domain, listed as unreachable on 06.09.2026 per the prior source, serves a working WooCommerce shop with no redirect as of 15.09.2026. The previously documented link farm with roughly 70 linked third-party shops could not be reproduced on the re-check; only three external domains remained on the homepage. The core legal shortcomings remain unchanged: legal notice, terms, and privacy policy are still missing, and ScamAdviser rates the domain accordingly low.

EuroPeptideShop was unreachable on 06.09.2026; on 15.09.2026 the site returns status 200 again. One single topic landing page now carries an RUO notice, while it is still missing from the homepage and the product page. A legal notice is still absent. ScamAdviser assigns a trust score of 0/100 ('may be a scam'). The product catalog is also technically broken: all 17 checked product links return a 404 error, so no purchase can be completed through any of them. The picture of a bare SEO facade without a working storefront holds up rather than dissolving.

Legal notices filled in

American Peptides was listed with no identifiable legal entity on 06.09.2026. As of 15.09.2026, the terms of service name the full company, American Peptides LLC, with address, phone number, and email, though still without a bundled EU-style legal notice page. A CoA lab is also now named (Bioviridian). This clarification raises the score from 42 to 62; details are on the American Peptides vendor page.

For Beyond Peptides, two findings combine: a new mandatory age gate before any shop access, confirmed by screenshot on 15.09.2026 (our 06.09.2026 baseline had flagged the missing age gate as a red flag), and an external lab verification via ChemVerify (Janoshik, MZ Biolabs). Legal entity, registered seat (Forgetrade Limited, Hong Kong), and shipping times remain unchanged. Altogether, the score rises from 52 to 80.

Restricted access: shipping and certificates

According to our 06.09.2026 baseline, Licensed Peptides shipped to the US, the EU, the UK, Australia, and New Zealand. The currently live shipping policy (dated 11.04.2026) names only the US as its shipping area. Whether the area actually shrank between the two check dates, or whether our baseline was imprecise on this point, cannot be conclusively resolved without further evidence; the database goes by the currently live policy. The score drops from 92 to 77 as a result.

Hydro Research Peptides had listed open access to its shop and certificates as a strength. A Wayback snapshot from 13.05.2026, however, already shows the same login requirement we found again on 15.09.2026: both /shop/ and /lab-results/ now require account creation, including age confirmation, before prices or CoA documents become visible. Trustpilot has also removed the vendor's profile over a policy violation. The score falls from 51 to 13.

Smart Peptides: an injection pen and a vanished review profile

As of our 06.09.2026 baseline, Smart Peptides merely advertised an injection pen. As of 15.09.2026, an actually orderable product appears: a prefilled syringe system including twelve sterile needles, compared directly in the body text to approved prefilled pens. Per the timestamps of a Reddit thread, a community observation, not independently verified, this product had already been in the catalog for roughly four weeks. This form of sale conflicts with the vendor's own RUO label, which remains present elsewhere on the site. This assessment is our team's interpretation, not a 1:1 finding of fact, and is being re-checked on an ongoing basis; see the Smart Peptides vendor page.

In addition, the Trustpilot profile for smartpeptides.de can no longer be found. This point does not factor into the score, since it matches none of our formula criteria, but we document it here as a fact regardless. The catalog itself has grown notably: over 48 products per the current sitemap, including new active substances and a new capsule line. Altogether, the overall score drops from 100 to 80.

More lab transparency, no effect on the score

Peptidwerk now shows a significantly expanded batch documentation: every checked product page carries a batch ID, the HPLC purity value, and for several products an endotoxin value as well, with a direct verification link to the Janoshik lab. The legal entity is now listed in the legal notice as a sole proprietorship. Since the lab and legal criteria already stood at the formula maximum before the update, the overall score stays unchanged at 100.

Particle Peptides has set up a public, searchable CoA vault with filtering by product, batch, and date. Product pages now also list endotoxin and heavy-metal values per batch, not just HPLC purity. Here too the score does not move; it already stood at 94, because the lab criterion was already rated at its maximum.

What this means for you

A score is a snapshot, not a guarantee. Within just nine days, the underlying facts actually changed for six of 24 vendors: domains moved, legal notices were filled in, shipping areas shrank. Before you rely on a claim about a specific vendor, it is worth checking that vendor's current detail page for the date of the last check.

Especially with domain changes like the one at Avellon Peptides: a familiar address does not necessarily still lead to the same vendor. When in doubt, re-check the legal notice and legal entity before assuming an existing connection to a shop still holds. We explain how to read a certificate of analysis yourself on the Verify a batch page; an overview of all 24 vendors is in the comparison.

Frequently asked questions

Why didn't Avellon Peptides' score drop to 0 even though the site is no longer reachable?
Because our formula does not artificially zero out the last documented values for inactive vendors; it keeps them and instead marks the vendor as not rankable. That way it stays traceable what the historical value was based on, without Avellon appearing in the current comparison.

Do you re-check every vendor on a regular basis?
Yes. This update from 15.09.2026 is the first re-check after our initial review on 06.09.2026. We repeat the check at regular intervals and publish every change with a source and a date, even when a value ends up unchanged.

What does it mean when a claim is labeled a community observation?
It means the information comes from a forum or a platform such as Reddit rather than the vendor's own site, a government source, or an established review platform. We treat such hints as an indicator, not a confirmed fact, and label them explicitly for that reason.
