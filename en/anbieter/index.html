How to choose a peptide vendor

Match the batch

A laboratory report should identify the sample and batch. A logo, a purity percentage or a report for a different product is not enough.

Check what was measured

Identity, purity and amount are different results. Read the method and date before drawing a conclusion from a headline number.

Compare the complete listing

Match compound, pack size, currency and additives. Check tax, shipping and destination restrictions separately.

Comparing vendors for the UK or US

A supplier’s location and its shipping destinations answer different questions. The UK and US filters show only destinations explicitly listed in our records. A missing destination means we have not established it; it does not prove that shipping is unavailable.

Before placing any research order, confirm whether that exact product can be shipped to your destination, who handles import requirements, and which taxes and delivery charges apply. A shipping claim does not establish legal eligibility or medical suitability. We have not validated these routes through test orders.

What our vendor reviews can establish

A documentation score summarises the criteria we have recorded. It is not a purity result, a safety rating or a guarantee that an order will arrive. Missing criteria must remain visible. Comparing a partial score with a complete record can otherwise create false confidence.

Questions about peptide suppliers

Which peptide vendor is best?

There is no universal winner established by our records. A useful comparison starts with your research specification and the available batch evidence. Price, laboratory documentation and delivery claims should be assessed separately.

Is third-party testing enough?

It is useful evidence only when the report is authentic, the sample is traceable and the measurements answer your question. A report does not establish every batch, every vial or suitability for human use.

Does an affiliate relationship change the review?

A commission is a commercial relationship, not laboratory evidence. Partner relationships are disclosed. Review the sources and gaps in each profile, including profiles of suppliers that are not partners.

Sources and record dates

Each profile links its underlying sources and displays the relevant dates. The comparison table is assembled from those existing records; it is not a fresh audit of every supplier on 1 November 2026. Where a profile contains a newer product or shipping check, that date applies only to the named observation.
