Is DN Lab Research legit?

The available record does not justify a blanket verdict. Check whether the business identity, current batch report and payment and delivery terms can be verified. A documented report supports only the sample and measurements it describes. Missing information remains an unresolved question.

How should I compare DN Lab Research with another supplier?

Use the same compound, declared amount and currency. Compare the report methods and batch identifiers separately from price. A cheaper listing, a discount code or a higher documentation score is not evidence of greater purity or safety.

Strengths

  • Domain registered since 2021-11-06 (WHOIS), a relatively long history for a peptide shop; valid SSL certificate (Google Trust Services). Source · 2026-09-15
  • Scam Detector rank 68.9/100, classification "Known. Vetted. Low Risk." Source · 2026-09-15
  • RUO disclaimer, 21+ age gate, and Research-Use-Only policy are consistently linked on the homepage, product pages, and a dedicated policy page. Source · Ad · 2026-09-15
  • 9 of the 12 researched core peptides are listed as standalone individual products with a clear mg amount in the 'Peptide Pen' format (not only as blends/bundles). Source · Ad · 2026-09-15

Limitations

  • No imprint, no company address, no commercial register or VAT ID reference findable anywhere on the website (Home, About, Contact, Terms of Sale, Disclaimer, RUO Policy checked). Source · Ad · 2026-09-15
  • CoA not publicly/externally linked: the product page literally states "Certificate of analysis (CoA) available for each batch upon request", despite the marketing badge '3rd Party Tested' directly next to it on the same page. Source · Ad · 2026-09-15
  • Contradictory WhatsApp contacts on the vendor's own site: the age-gate popup (homepage + product pages) shows +34 661516922 (Spanish country code), while the contact page separately names "Customer Services – Whatsapp Only" +971 50 171 9355 (UAE country code). Source · Ad · 2026-09-15
  • Jurisdiction in the Terms of Sale remains undefined: "Law of the Province of business registration of Dnlab Research shall apply", naming no country or province, reading like unadapted boilerplate. Source · Ad · 2026-09-15
  • Different legal-entity name in the Research-Use-Only policy: "All products supplied by Bioniq Lab are provided strictly for research...", deviating from the name "Dnlab Research"/"DN Research" used consistently elsewhere; additionally a product category "Bioniq Lab" exists in the shop. Source · Ad · 2026-09-15
  • The earlier location statement "Farnborough, UK" (as of 2026-09-06, per the footer) is no longer findable on any of the currently checked pages (Home, About, Contact, Terms, Disclaimer, RUO): the only tangible contact trace is the WhatsApp number with the UAE country code, which supports the Dubai theory (peptidcheck24) more than the UK theory. A complete site crawl was not possible (wp-sitemap 404), hence stated as 'no longer findable', not claimed as 'removed'. Source · Ad · 2026-09-15
  • ScamAdviser shows contradictory signals simultaneously: the label 'Likely Safe', but at the same time a 'Trust Score 0'; the value is presumably distorted by a JS-rendering gap in the server-side fetch, and is therefore not clearly usable. Source · 2026-09-15
  • The GLP-1 class is missing under recognized names: Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, and Retatrutide are not findable via the shop search (Store API). Instead, a product "GLP-3 | 30mg Peptide Pen" (EUR 340) is offered: not a recognized active-ingredient name, and its composition was not checked in detail on the product page. Source · Ad · 2026-09-15
  • The homepage and product pages advertise a "30 Minute Coaching Call" with named individuals (Tom Kinsey, Dean Henry as "health entrepreneur"/"peptide specialist") who, in an RUO context, reference individual consultation/protocols: borderline close to human-use implications despite the RUO disclaimer. Classifying this as an HWG (German advertising-for-medicinal-products law) risk is interpretation; the underlying observation itself is documented. Source · Ad · 2026-09-15
