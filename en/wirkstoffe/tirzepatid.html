Tirzepatide: research, prices and suppliers

A dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist, known as an approved medication and listed here as a research substance for metabolic regulation.

Sources and dates are stated with individual entries. This new presentation is not a complete re-investigation of all data.

FactsEvidenceFrequently asked questionsDocumented pricesQuestions about interpretation

Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist approved as a medication under the names Mounjaro and Zepbound. It is the subject of extensive clinical research on metabolic and weight regulation in type 2 diabetes and obesity. The research batches listed here are not equivalent to approved medications and are intended for laboratory use only.

The references below describe different investigations. Cell or animal findings do not establish benefits in humans. A clinical study concerns the tested preparation, population and study design, not automatically a shop product.

Facts

  • CAS number: 2023788-19-2
  • Molecular weight: 4813 g/mol
  • Amino acids: 39
  • Half-life: ≈ 5 days (elimination, Mounjaro prescribing information)
  • Purity specification: ≥ 99 % (HPLC, commercial standard)

Evidence

  • In a randomized trial with 2,539 adults with obesity or overweight, weekly tirzepatide over 72 weeks led to a mean body weight loss of up to 20.9 percent (15 mg) versus 3.1 percent under placebo. Jastreboff AM et al. 2022, New England Journal of Medicine, PMID 35658024
  • In a randomized head-to-head trial with 1,879 people with type 2 diabetes, tirzepatide lowered HbA1c by 2.09 to 2.46 percentage points and body weight by 7.8 to 12.4 kg after 40 weeks, in each case more than semaglutide 1 mg. Frías JP et al. 2021, New England Journal of Medicine, PMID 34170647

Frequently asked questions

Is tirzepatide an approved medication?


Yes, tirzepatide is approved as a prescription medication under the names Mounjaro and Zepbound. The substance described here is a research batch only, not a finished pharmaceutical product.

How does tirzepatide differ from semaglutide?


Semaglutide acts only on the GLP-1 receptor, while tirzepatide additionally acts on the GIP receptor. In a head-to-head trial, tirzepatide showed larger effects on HbA1c and body weight than semaglutide.

What is tirzepatide used for as a medication?


As an approved medication, tirzepatide is prescribed for type 2 diabetes and weight management. This page describes the substance exclusively as a research chemical for laboratory purposes.
