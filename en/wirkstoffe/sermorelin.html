Sermorelin: research, prices and suppliers

A short-chain fragment of the body's own GHRH, historically approved as a medication, now sold as a research substance.

Sources and dates are stated with individual entries. This new presentation is not a complete re-investigation of all data.

FactsEvidenceFrequently asked questionsDocumented pricesQuestions about interpretation

Sermorelin is the biologically active fragment GHRH(1-29) of the body's own growth hormone-releasing hormone. In the US it was approved in 1990 as a diagnostic agent and in 1997 for growth hormone deficiency-related short stature in children; the approval was withdrawn in 2008 for manufacturing reasons. Today it is studied outside that historical approval as a research substance.

The references below describe different investigations. Cell or animal findings do not establish benefits in humans. A clinical study concerns the tested preparation, population and study design, not automatically a shop product.

Facts

  • CAS number: 86168-78-7
  • Molecular weight: 3357.9 g/mol
  • Amino acids: 29
  • Purity specification: ≥ 98 % (HPLC, commercial standard)
  • Solubility: soluble in water and dilute acetic acid

Evidence

  • In a randomized, placebo-controlled trial in older men and women, an Nle27-substituted GHRH(1-29) analog raised growth hormone and IGF-1 levels over several months and improved fat-free body mass. Khorram et al. 1997, Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism, PMID 9141536
  • A review of the available clinical data in children with growth hormone deficiency described accelerated growth velocity over twelve months in most of the children studied. Prakash & Goa 1999, BioDrugs, PMID 18031173

Frequently asked questions

Was Sermorelin once an approved medication


Yes. Sermorelin was approved in the US from 1990 to 2008, first as a diagnostic agent, later for growth hormone deficiency-related short stature in children. The approval was withdrawn for manufacturing reasons, not safety concerns.

What is the difference between sermorelin and CJC-1295


Both are GHRH analogs. Sermorelin consists of the first 29 amino acids of the body's own GHRH with no further modification, while CJC-1295 is additionally stabilized and therefore has a longer half-life.

Is sermorelin available as a medication today


No. Since the market withdrawal in 2008, sermorelin is no longer available as an approved medication in the EU or the US and is sold as a research chemical.
