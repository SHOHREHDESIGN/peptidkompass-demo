CJC-1295: research, prices and suppliers

A synthetic analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone, studied in two variants with different durations of action.

Sources and dates are stated with individual entries. This new presentation is not a complete re-investigation of all data.

EvidenceFrequently asked questionsDocumented pricesQuestions about interpretation

CJC-1295 is a stabilized analog of the body's own GHRH, studied in the context of the growth hormone axis. It exists in two variants: without DAC (Drug Affinity Complex), which has a short duration of action, and with DAC, which achieves a substantially extended duration through binding to serum albumin. Available human data come mainly from early clinical research in healthy adults.

The references below describe different investigations. Cell or animal findings do not establish benefits in humans. A clinical study concerns the tested preparation, population and study design, not automatically a shop product.

Evidence

  • Two randomized, placebo-controlled trials in healthy adults with step-wise increasing amounts: a single injection of CJC-1295 with DAC raised growth hormone levels for several days and IGF-1 levels for 9 to 11 days. Teichman et al. 2006, Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism, PMID 16352683
  • A follow-up analysis of the same subjects found that pulsatile growth hormone release was preserved even under the extended stimulation from CJC-1295 with DAC. Ionescu & Frohman 2006, Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism, PMID 17018654

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CJC-1295 with and without DAC


Without DAC, the peptide is broken down within minutes, producing a short, pronounced hormone pulse. With DAC, a chemical anchor binds to albumin in the blood, extending the duration to several days, while studies indicate the pulsatile release pattern is preserved.

Is CJC-1295 an approved medication


No. CJC-1295 is not approved as a medication in the EU or the US and is sold exclusively as a research chemical for laboratory use.

What is CJC-1295 studied for in research


CJC-1295 is studied in the context of the growth hormone axis, particularly regarding the body's own release of growth hormone and the resulting IGF-1 production in the liver.
