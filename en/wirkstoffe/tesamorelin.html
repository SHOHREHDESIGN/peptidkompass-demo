Tesamorelin: research, prices and suppliers

A stabilized GHRH analog, approved in the US as the medication Egrifta for reducing visceral abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy.

Sources and dates are stated with individual entries. This new presentation is not a complete re-investigation of all data.

FactsEvidenceFrequently asked questionsDocumented pricesQuestions about interpretation

Tesamorelin is a stabilized analog of the full 44-amino-acid GHRH. Under the brand name Egrifta, it has been approved by the US FDA since 2010 for reducing visceral abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy. This makes it the only peptide in its class with a completed phase 3 approval study in more than 800 participants.

The references below describe different investigations. Cell or animal findings do not establish benefits in humans. A clinical study concerns the tested preparation, population and study design, not automatically a shop product.

Facts

  • CAS number: 218949-48-5
  • Molecular weight: 5136 g/mol
  • Amino acids: 44
  • Half-life: ≈ 8 minutes (elimination, Egrifta SV prescribing information)
  • Purity specification: ≥ 98 % (HPLC, commercial standard)

Evidence

  • In a randomized, placebo-controlled phase 3 trial in people with HIV-associated fat redistribution, tesamorelin reduced visceral fat tissue significantly more than placebo over 26 weeks and raised IGF-1 levels. Falutz et al. 2007, New England Journal of Medicine, PMID 18057338
  • A further randomized trial in HIV patients with abdominal fat accumulation confirmed the reduction in visceral fat tissue and additionally showed a decrease in liver fat. Stanley et al. 2014, JAMA, PMID 25038357

Frequently asked questions

Is tesamorelin an approved medication


Yes, under the name Egrifta since 2010 in the US, but only for a specific indication: reducing visceral abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy. There is no approval in the EU.

Does tesamorelin build muscle


Studies show an increase in fat-free body mass, but a relevant share of this consists of water and connective tissue. Notable muscle growth without additional androgen receptor stimulation is not scientifically documented.

How does tesamorelin differ from other GHRH analogs


Tesamorelin is the only GHRH analog with a completed phase 3 approval study in more than 800 participants and an active regulatory approval in the US. Sermorelin and CJC-1295 have not reached this status.
